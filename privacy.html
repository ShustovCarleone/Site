Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Overview

ShuGhost respects your privacy. This page explains what the website stores and what happens when you open external services.

Information stored on your device

The website stores only your selected language in local browser storage. This setting remains on your device and is not sent to ShuGhost.

Personal data

This website does not create user accounts, process payments, collect form submissions, or store personal profiles.

Anonymous visitor counter

The site creates a random ID for the current browser tab to calculate anonymous online and total view counts. The ID expires with the browser session. The server uses the network address temporarily to prevent duplicate views and abuse, but does not save it in the visitor statistics file.

External services

Links to Steam, Twitch, Telegram, Discord, Ko-fi, DonationAlerts, and email services open third-party websites. Their own privacy policies apply after you leave this website.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact shughostcom@gmail.com.
